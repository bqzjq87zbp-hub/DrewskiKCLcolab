## Import contract

`scripts/expansion.py` accepts a private JSON manifest with schema `kcl-curated-expansion-v1`, a batch identity, and reviewed items. Each item supplies a stable photo ID, original photographic identity, category, literal title/alt, original hash and orientation-correct display dimensions, full/thumbnail paths and hashes and dimensions, reviewer/evidence, and full-frame/category/truthful-role confirmations. `public_status` is explicit; eligible professional work references the existing use authority. A local review decision is not publication.

Dry-run is the default:

```sh
python3 scripts/expansion.py --manifest "$CURATED_MANIFEST" \
  --allow-root "$ORIGINALS_ROOT" --allow-root "$DERIVATIVES_ROOT" \
  --ledger "$PRIVATE_LEDGER"
```

Add `--apply` only in the isolated review checkout. The ledger must remain outside the entire repository. Optional `placement.after_id` inserts within a category while preserving the accepted relative order. Repeating the same applied manifest is a no-op; changed evidence, missing anchors, altered originals/exports, exact duplicate images and unexpected category drift fail. Interrupted writes use a private journal and recover on the next explicit apply.

Budgets per batch: at most 48 entries and 64 MiB of new derivatives. Thumbnails are at most 1200 pixels on the long edge and 350 KiB; full files at most 24 megapixels and 8 MiB. Supplied exports retain the original aspect ratio with rounding tolerance. The importer does not edit photographs, develop RAW, crop, upscale or produce synthetic image detail.
